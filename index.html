The fix is in the empty seats

One cab instead of three

A cab carrying one rider has three empty seats. When three people going the same way fill them, the same trip needs one cab instead of three: about a third of the fare each, and roughly a third of the emissions per person.

Your impact, counted

How it works

Four steps from solo cab to shared ride

  1. Say where you're going

    Pick your drop and when you're leaving: right now, or later. Your pickup is where you are.

  2. Join a ride, or start one

    See rides already going your way, the shortest walk first, and join in a tap. Nothing fits? Start one, and anyone searching your route sees it.

  3. Meet at the pickup point

    One meeting spot per ride. Tap "I'm here" so everyone knows who has arrived.

  4. Book one cab, split the fare

    One person books the Ola, Uber or auto. Everyone settles their share by UPI or cash.

Two kinds of ride

Across town, or out of town

Within the city

  • Rides that start a short walk from you and end a short walk from your drop.
  • You choose how far you'll walk to meet: 5, 10, 15 or 20 minutes.
  • Leave right now, or plan a ride for later.
  • The fare is split equally, by head.

Outstation

  • Longer trips out of town, in one cab booked for the whole way.
  • Join a ride whose route passes near you, even if it starts across town.
  • Get on and off along the way, wherever the route suits you.
  • The fare is split by how far each rider travels.

Sobat only helps you find each other

We don't run cabs, employ drivers or handle your money. You book your own ride and settle the fare with your group directly. Our part ends once your group has met.

Safety

Built for riding with people you haven't met yet

Rides, not profiles

Before you join, you see where a ride goes, when it leaves and how it's rated, never who's in it.

Real accounts

Every rider has an account with their name and a mobile number. Other riders never see your number.

Women-only rides

Women can choose rides where every rider is a woman. Sobat enforces it on its servers, not just in the app.

Ratings both ways

Riders rate each other after every trip, so no-shows and bad behaviour become visible.

No phone numbers shared

Coordinate in the ride's chat, with quick replies. Your number stays private.

Report and block

Report anyone who makes you uncomfortable, and block them from ever matching with you again.

Questions

Good to know

How do I share a cab with someone going the same way?

Tell Sobat where you're going. It shows rides already heading there at about the same time, the shortest walk first. Join one in a tap, or start your own so others going your way can join. Then meet at the pickup point and book a single cab together.

How is the cab fare split?

Within the city, equally per person by default, so three riders each pay a third. On outstation rides, by how far each rider travels. Anyone can change their own share, for example if they get off early. You settle the fare with your group by UPI or cash; Sobat never handles money.

Is sharing a cab with strangers safe?

Every rider has an account with their name and a mobile number that other riders never see. Riders rate each other after every ride, and everyone meets at a public pickup point. Before you join, you see the ride, not the people. You coordinate in the ride's chat without sharing phone numbers, and you can report or block anyone.

Can I share a cab for an outstation trip?

Yes. Choose Outstation when you search. Sobat shows rides whose route passes near your pickup and your drop, even if they start somewhere else, and splits the fare by how far each person travels.

Can women choose women-only rides?

Yes. Women can choose rides where every rider is a woman, and Sobat enforces this on its servers, not just in the app.

Is Sobat a carpool app?

Not quite. Carpool apps connect you with someone driving their own car. On Sobat nobody drives anyone: people going the same way share a regular Ola, Uber or auto that one of them books.

Where does Sobat work?

Anywhere in India. Search from wherever you are. The more people near you use Sobat, the quicker you'll find someone going your way, so tell your colleagues and friends.

Does Sobat book the cab for us?

No. One person in your group books a normal Ola, Uber or auto in their own app. Sobat never sees or handles the fare.
