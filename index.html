The fix is in the empty seats

One cab instead of three

A cab carrying one rider has three empty seats. When three people going the same way fill them, the same trip needs one cab instead of three: about a third of the fare each, and roughly a third of the emissions per person.

How it works

Four steps from solo cab to shared ride

  1. Post your trip

    Set your pickup, your drop, and when you're leaving. Right now, or later today.

  2. Find people going your way

    See rides heading to the same area at the same time, and choose one to join.

  3. Meet at the pickup point

    One meeting spot per ride. Tap "I'm here" so everyone knows who has arrived.

  4. Book one cab, split the fare

    One person books the Ola, Uber or auto. Everyone settles their share by UPI or cash.

Sobat only helps you find each other

We don't run cabs, employ drivers or handle your money. You book your own ride and settle the fare with your group directly. Our part ends once your group has met.

Safety

Built for riding with people you haven't met yet

Verified riders

Every rider is verified before their first shared ride. One account per person.

Women-only rides

Women can choose rides where every rider is a woman.

Ratings both ways

Riders rate each other after every trip, so no-shows and bad behaviour become visible.

No phone numbers shared

Coordinate in the ride's group chat. Your number stays private.

One meeting point

Everyone meets at the same public spot, with a live board showing who has arrived.

Report and block

Report anyone who makes you uncomfortable, and block them from ever matching with you again.

Questions

Good to know

Where does Sobat work?

Anywhere in India. Post a trip from wherever you are. The more people near you use Sobat, the quicker you'll find someone going your way, so tell your colleagues and friends.

Does Sobat book the cab for us?

No. One person in your group books a normal Ola, Uber or auto in their own app. Sobat never sees or handles the fare.
