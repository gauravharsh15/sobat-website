Who we are

Sobat is a meeting layer for shared cabs — we help people going the same way find each other, meet up, and split a fare they arrange and pay for themselves. We don’t operate vehicles, employ drivers, or process payments. Sobat is operated by [Company/Entity Name], [registered address].

What we collect

To run the matching and safety features described below, we collect:

  • Your phone number and email address.
  • Your first and last name, profile photo, date of birth and gender. Riders you share a ride with see your first name, photo and an age range worked out from your date of birth. They never see your last name, date of birth, phone number or email.
  • A coarse profession category, if you choose to add one.
  • Pickup and drop points for rides you start or join, and your device location when you use “current location” or look for rides leaving near you.
  • The routes people search, kept only as a daily count per area of about 1 km, with nothing that identifies who searched.
  • If you verify your identity: the result from our licensed verification provider (pass or fail only). We never store your ID number or a copy of your ID document.
  • Messages you send in a ride’s chat, ratings you give or receive, and reports you file.
  • A push-notification token (phone apps) or your browser’s notification address (web app), if you allow notifications.
  • Whether you want Sobat’s launch news by email (the switch at sign-up). You can unsubscribe at any time, in one click, from any of our emails.
  • If you look around without signing in: your network (IP) address, only in scrambled form and only to stop automated abuse. It’s deleted within two days and never linked to an account.

What stays on your phone

Your saved Home and Work places and your recent places are stored only on your phone. They’re never uploaded. We only see a place when you search for, start or join a ride with it.

Why we collect it

Only to show you rides going your way, put you in a ride with compatible riders, help you meet up, notify you about your rides, let riders rate each other, and contact you about your account or a safety report. We don’t use your data for advertising, and we don’t sell it.

Who sees what

Before you join a ride, other riders see only facts about it: where and when it goes, how many seats are left, and its riders’ average rating. They never see who is in it. Once you’re in a ride together, its riders see each other’s first name, photo, age range, profession and rating. A ride’s chat is visible only to its riders.

Safety reviews

When a rider files a report, a small number of Sobat staff review it. To do that they can see the reported ride’s chat and riders, and the ride history, ratings and past reports of the people involved. Phone numbers and email addresses stay hidden unless a reviewer needs to contact someone about the report, and each time one is shown, that is recorded. Staff can warn a rider, suspend an account for a set time or until further notice (a suspended rider can’t start or join rides or chat), cancel a ride used for abuse, or delete an account when the law requires it. Staff sign in with two-step verification.

Every action our staff take is written to an audit log: who did it, what they did, to which account or ride, why, when, and a scrambled form of their network address. Nobody can change or delete its entries, including us. If an account is deleted, the log keeps only an internal account number, not the person’s name or contact details, so we can show how a safety or legal matter was handled.

Emails we send

We email you about your account only: a welcome when you finish signing up, and a last note confirming it when your account is deleted. If you chose launch news, you get that too, and each of those emails has a one-click unsubscribe link that works without signing in. Our emails have no tracking: no images or pixels that tell us you opened them, and no tracked links. We send them with Google Workspace (Gmail), from noreply@getsobat.in; replies go to support@getsobat.in.

When a rider files a report, our team gets an email saying so, with a link to it, but without anyone’s name or what the report says. If you unsubscribe, we keep your address, marked as unsubscribed, only so we never email it again.

Who we share it with

We use a few service providers to run the app, each processing only what it needs: Supabase (our database, sign-in and live updates), WhatsApp from Meta (sending verification codes), our SMS provider (verification codes), Google (Google sign-in and place search), your phone’s own maps service from Apple or Google (to name a point you drop on the map), OpenStreetMap (maps in the web app), Cloudflare (a bot check on our website’s forms and web sign-in), Expo (push notifications on phones), your browser’s push service (notifications in the web app), GitHub (hosting this website), and, once available, our identity verification provider. None of them may use your data for their own purposes.

How long we keep it

  • A ride’s chat is deleted 30 days after the ride ends, unless a report about that ride is still open: then it’s kept until the report has been handled.
  • Entries in our staff audit log are kept for [audit log retention period].
  • Exact pickup and drop points are rounded to an area of about 1 km 30 days after a ride ends. We keep that area-level history for your ride list and our statistics.
  • Your account and everything linked to it is deleted when you delete your account.
  • Copies of the emails we send are deleted after 7 days. The one confirming an account’s deletion is deleted as soon as it’s sent.

Website and newsletter

If you subscribe to updates on this website, or leave the newsletter switch on when you sign up in the app, we store your email address only to send you news about Sobat, such as when its apps launch. You can unsubscribe at any time. This website doesn’t use advertising or third-party analytics. It counts page views itself: which page, the site that linked to it (its domain only) and whether it’s a phone, tablet or computer, as daily totals with no cookies, no IP addresses and nothing that identifies you. Its newsletter and phone-code forms use Cloudflare Turnstile, a bot check that reads signals from your browser only to tell people from bots.

Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us what data we hold about you, correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can delete your account and all associated data yourself at any time from Profile → Delete account in the app, or on our website at getsobat.in/delete-account.html, or contact us at [grievance officer email].

Age

Sobat is for users 18 and older. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone younger.
