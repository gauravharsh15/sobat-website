What Sobat is

Sobat helps people going the same way find each other and split a cab fare. We are not a ride-hailing company: we have no drivers, no vehicles, and we never process any payment for your ride. One of you books a normal cab or auto, and you settle the fare with your co-riders directly (UPI, cash, however you agree).

Where our responsibility ends

Our part is helping you find compatible riders and meet up safely. Once your group has met and booked a ride, that ride is between you, your co-riders and the driver, the same as it would be for any group of friends sharing a cab. We don’t track, monitor or take responsibility for what happens during the ride itself.

Who can use Sobat

You must be 18 or older, provide accurate information, and verify your mobile number. We may ask you to verify your identity through a licensed provider. One account per person.

How to behave

Show up when you say you will. Be honest about your pickup, drop, and how many people you’re bringing. Treat other riders with respect: harassment, threats or discrimination have no place here and will get your account removed. Report anyone who makes you feel unsafe using the report option in the ride room.

Fare

Any fare split shown in the app is informational only, based on what riders enter. It is not a bill, an invoice, or a guarantee of the actual fare. Settling the fare is entirely between you and your co-riders.
